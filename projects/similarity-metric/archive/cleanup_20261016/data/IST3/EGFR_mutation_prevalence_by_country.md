# EGFR Mutation Prevalence in NSCLC by Country/Region

## Overview

EGFR mutation is a confirmed effect modifier for gefitinib efficacy in NSCLC (Mok et al. 2009, IPASS trial).
This document compiles country-level EGFR mutation prevalence data from published systematic reviews and
large epidemiological studies, for use as a case study demonstrating the nABCD distribution similarity metric.

## Key Observations for nABCD Case Study

1. **Wide global variation**: 2% (Saudi Arabia) to 67% (Peru), ideal for demonstrating sensitivity of nABCD
2. **Within-region variation**: Even within Asia, ranges from 40% (Singapore) to 64% (Vietnam)
3. **Clear ethnic/geographic gradient**: Asia (~45-50%) >> Americas (~20-30%) >> Europe (~10-15%)
4. **Clinical relevance**: This variation directly impacts gefitinib efficacy expectations across MRCT regions
5. **Binary EM**: EGFR mutation is binary (mutant vs wild-type), simplifying the distribution to Bernoulli
6. **Rich data**: 43 countries with published prevalence data, enabling diverse pairwise comparisons
7. **For Bernoulli distribution**: nABCD reduces to a function of |p1 - p2|, making interpretation straightforward
